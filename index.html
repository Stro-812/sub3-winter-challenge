One season. Twelve weekly stages. Multiple running challenges.

Train and compete

Participants connect Garmin, Coros, Polar or Strava and take part in different running challenges on s10.run. The platform supports runners of different levels and tracks activity and progress through challenge formats and leaderboards.

Track progress

Participants follow their progress through the challenge and compare results on the leaderboard.

Challenge leaderboards

Each challenge has its own format, stages and leaderboard, with results determined by the relevant challenge rules.

The s10.run platform

s10.run has operated online running challenges since 2020 and has a community of 50,000+ members. Its autumn 2026 tournament recorded about 8,000 participants across 13 stages.
